# chitkara-campus-hole-game

eat my university

A 3D hole.io-style game set on the real Chitkara University, Himachal Pradesh campus — Baddi.

You control a black hole. Eat small things first, grow, then eat bigger things — bikes, cars, buses, and finally the buildings themselves.

## Real campus, not a random map

Campus: 17 acres, Atal Shiksha Kunj, Barotiwala, Baddi, HP — 32 km from Chandigarh, 12 km from Pinjore.

Buildings and places in the game use their real names:

- Academic Block
- Kalam Block (gym inside it)
- Pharma Block
- Tagore Library — 3-storey, reading hall 9am–9pm, 395 seats
- Bose Hostel — 2nd year onwards, 5 floors
- Aryabhatta Hostel — 1st year, 5 floors
- Boys hostels (4) and girls hostels (4) — 8 total
- Football ground, basketball court, volleyball court, kho-kho ground, badminton courts, chess area

No Square One and no cricket ground — those are not verified for the Baddi campus, so they are not in the game.

Buses run to Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula, Pinjore, Kalka, Parwanoo, Derabassi, Baddi and Nalagarh. Vehicles park outside the campus — the map is compact, like the real campus.

Building positions inside the campus are approximate. The game uses stylised 3D models labelled with the real names, not photos of every building.

## How to play

- Move the hole with WASD / arrow keys, mouse drag, or touch drag.
- Eat what fits in the hole. The hole grows as you eat.
- 2-minute timer. Score for everything you swallow.
- Small first: trees, benches, dustbins, bikes. Then cars and buses. Then buildings when you are big enough.
